03 / CONTROL FRAMEWORK

Model risk considerations

A bank deployment would require data completeness, time-of-prediction leakage testing, asymmetric error review, backtesting, challenger models and independent validation.

Not a production validated model

From ML prototype to ALM decision support

Keep the prediction engine separate from liquidity aggregation, risk thresholds and human-authorized actions. For a client implementation, connect approved data feeds and validate performance against actual value-dated settlements.
