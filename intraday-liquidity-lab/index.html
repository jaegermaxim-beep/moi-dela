      07 / MODEL GOVERNANCE

      Transparent by design

      The prototype distinguishes measured synthetic results from assumptions and future production requirements.

      01 / TRAINING

      Supervised Random Forest

      32 regression trees, trained on 16,500 completely simulated payments across six synthetic products and four payment rails.

      Target: minutes from booking to settlement
      02 / VALIDATION

      Chronological holdout

      First 80% of synthetic days for training and last 20% for testing. The rules baseline uses training-period group medians only.

      — synthetic test transactions
      03 / CONTROL FRAMEWORK

      Model risk considerations

      A bank deployment would require data completeness, time-of-prediction leakage testing, asymmetric error review, backtesting, challenger models and independent validation.

      Not a production validated model

      From ML prototype to ALM decision support

      Keep the prediction engine separate from liquidity aggregation, risk thresholds and human-authorized actions. For a client implementation, connect approved data feeds and validate performance against actual value-dated settlements.
